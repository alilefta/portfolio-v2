## What it is

Freelancer Command Center is an in-development workspace for independent professionals who need a clearer view of the work behind their income. It brings projects, time, invoices, and cash flow into one practical operating surface.

## Why it matters

Freelancers often lose time switching between task lists, timers, spreadsheets, and payment tools. This project is designed to make the important questions easy to answer: What is moving? What needs attention? What has been billed? What is still outstanding?

## Current direction

The current product direction focuses on a calm dashboard, explicit project states, and useful financial signals. The interface is intentionally being developed around the real decisions a solo operator makes each week, rather than a generic analytics wall.

## Scope in progress

- Project and client workspace
- Time and workload tracking
- Invoice lifecycle and payment status
- Cash-flow overview
- A focused list of next actions

This is an active build. The visual is a product direction mockup, not a claim that every planned capability is already shipped.
